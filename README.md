# Financial Health Dashboard

## overview

a financial analysis dashboard built with power bi to evaluate company financial performance, profitability, financial stability, cash flow, shareholder equity, and revenue forecasting.

## key metrics

- revenue: 12.21m
- average roe: 12.43%
- average roa: 7.78%
- ebitda: 3.25m
- net income: 1.98m
- profit margin: 16.2%

## dashboard sections

### financial health summary

- profit margin trend
- revenue and net income trend
- companies profit comparison
- revenue, gross profit, and net income comparison

### financial position, cash flow & forecasting

- financial stability analysis
- shareholder equity by company
- cash flow comparison by company
- revenue forecast

## key insights

- revenue reached 12.21m with a net income of 1.98m.
- the average roe is 12.43%, while the average roa is 7.78%.
- aapl has the highest displayed company profit at approximately 0.68m.
- goog has the highest displayed shareholder equity at approximately 1.92m.
- the dashboard compares operating, investing, and financing cash flows across companies.
- revenue forecasting extends the analysis beyond historical performance and provides a projected future trend.
